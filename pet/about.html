About CuddlePaws

Where Every Pet is Treated
Like Family

Soft baths, gentle hands, and lots of love since 2015. We’re a small team of certified groomers who believe grooming should feel like a spa day.

Our Story

Born from a Love of Fluffy Friends

CuddlePaws started as a tiny home-based setup with one very enthusiastic groomer and a lot of wet towels. Today we’re a calm, purpose-built studio in Ratanada where dogs and cats leave happier than they arrived.

We specialise in gentle, low-stress grooming. Every session is tailored to your pet’s personality, coat type, and comfort level — whether they’re a bouncing puppy or a senior cat who just wants a quiet brush.

  • ♡ Stress-free environment — calm music, treats & endless patience
  • ♡ Pet-safe, natural products — gentle on skin & coat
  • ♡ Certified, animal-loving groomers who treat every pet like their own
