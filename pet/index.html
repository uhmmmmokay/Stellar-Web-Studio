About CuddlePaws

Where Every Pet is Treated Like Family

At CuddlePaws, we believe grooming should feel like a spa day, not a chore. Our gentle, patient team uses only pet-safe products and lots of love to keep your furry friends looking and feeling their absolute best.

From fluffy Pomeranians to elegant Persian cats, we tailor every session to your pet’s personality, coat type, and comfort level.

  • ♡ Stress-free environment — calm music, treats & patience
  • ♡ Pet-safe, natural products — gentle on skin & coat
  • ♡ Experienced, certified groomers who truly love animals
